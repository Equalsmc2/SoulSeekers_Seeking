# SoulSeekers: Seeking — Gold-first UI visual guide (R03.4.2)

Reference: SoulSeekers website V40.10.

- Primary accent: **Gold** `#d9b45f`; highlighted gold `#f4d98b`; antique/deep gold `#97752f`.
- Surfaces: charcoal `#0b0a0d` and ink `#17151b`; type on dark: ivory `#f4f0e7`.
- Red remains only for vital signs/health, danger and error states. It is **not** the brand accent for menus, selection, HUD edges, or DM controls.
- Headings use condensed editorial lettering, asymmetrical cuts, gold dividers and SoulSeekers crest. Avoid heavy animated filters that could harm 60fps gameplay.
- Hero identity colors may still differentiate individual members, and enemy team colors are not overridden by the UI palette.
- The city map is procedural/native, **not Kenney tiled**. The two large playable jungle regions and routes remain unmodified.
