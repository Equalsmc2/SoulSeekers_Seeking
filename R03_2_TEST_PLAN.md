# R03.2 — Map Art Acceptance Checklist

1. Open the deployed game, confirm `PROTOTYPE R03.2 · KENNEY CITY ART`.
2. Start Party training; confirm road, roof, pavement and foliage sprites appear.
3. Move through the north jungle and south jungle; test cross-town shortcuts.
4. Confirm building, street car and cover collisions match what is drawn.
5. Verify the six fort bosses, DM tower, 16 chests and two shops are accessible.
6. Host a room on Render and join as a Party player. Both must see the same art
   without a mismatch in damage, pathing, or movement.
7. Test DM possession and enemy march orders across both jungles.
8. Compare 60fps/P95 frame time to R03.1, especially on older computers.

The existing 9 local suites cover gameplay and startup, not live Render load.
